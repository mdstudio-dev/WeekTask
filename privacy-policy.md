4. Android Device Permissions Requested
WeekTask requests the following permissions on your Android device to function properly:
Permission
Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET
Required to request and display advertisements via Google AdMob and process consent status.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Allows the app to verify active internet connectivity before loading ad content.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID
Required on Android 13+ for Google AdMob SDK to access the device's Advertising ID for ad targeting and frequency capping.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Required on Android 13+ to send local task reminder notifications scheduled by the user.
android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Allows the app to trigger task notification alarms precisely at the user's chosen time.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Used to automatically restore and reschedule your task alarms after your device is restarted.

You have the right to change or withdraw your consent choices at any time directly within the Application:
1.
Open WeekTask.
2.
Open Settings (gear icon).
3.
Scroll to the Privacy section and tap "Manage Ad Privacy Settings".

The Google UMP Privacy Form will reopen, allowing you to modify or revoke your consent preferences.
Alternatively, you can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your Advertising ID on your Android device by going to: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
