Overview & Privacy Guarantee
At ouayat studio, we value your privacy. WeekTask is designed to operate primarily as an offline, on-device productivity tool.
•
No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use WeekTask.
•
100% On-Device Task Storage: All your planned tasks, schedules, categories, subtasks, priorities, and app settings (such as accent color and language preferences) are stored strictly locally on your Android device.
2. Information We Collect and Process
A. Personal Information
We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
B. Local Data Storage
All user-entered data (tasks, reminders, schedule grids) is stored using Android's local DataStore / SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application or clear app storage.
3. Third-Party Advertising & Privacy SDKs
WeekTask uses standard Google SDKs to serve advertisements and manage privacy compliance:
A. Google AdMob
•
We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements.
•
AdMob may collect and process anonymous technical data, such as your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, app interaction data, and device characteristics to deliver ads.
•
Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
B. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
•
In regions requiring explicit privacy consent (such as the European Economic Area / EEA and UK under GDPR), WeekTask uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
•
UMP allows you to review, grant, or revoke consent for personalized advertising at any time through the Privacy Settings menu inside the app.
4. Android Device Permissions
WeekTask requests only the necessary Android permissions required for its feature set:
•
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Google AdMob ad requests and consent status checks.
•
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to deliver task reminder notifications on Android 13+ (API 33+).
•
android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to schedule precise task alarms and reminders.
•
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to restore task notifications after your device reboots.
•
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Required by Google AdMob to deliver advertising on SDK 33+.
5. Children’s Privacy
WeekTask does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective steps.
6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal or operational requirements. Any updates will be posted with an updated Effective Date at the top of this document.
